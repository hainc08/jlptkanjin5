Kanji N5 is a kanji flashcard quiz app for Vietnamese learners preparing for the JLPT N5 exam. The interface is in Vietnamese.

No login or account is required. All features are available right after launch:
1. The splash screen opens the quiz automatically after about 2 seconds.
2. Tap one of the 4 answers. The card flips to show the Sino-Vietnamese reading, meaning, On and Kun readings. Tap "Câu tiếp theo" (Next) to continue.
3. After the last question, the Result screen shows the score. "Ôn câu sai" replays only the wrong answers; "Làm bài mới" starts a new quiz.
4. The gear icon opens Settings: number of questions, question type, dark mode, hints and auto-next.

Ads: the app shows Google AdMob banner ads (Result and Settings screens) and an occasional interstitial after finishing a quiz. Consent for personalized ads and the App Tracking Transparency prompt are handled by Google's User Messaging Platform. The app itself does not collect personal data; settings are stored only on the device.
