CHANGES SINCE THE 4.3 / 4.2.6 REJECTION
We previously submitted five apps (Kanji N5, N4, N3, N2, N1) that shared one codebase and differed only in their kanji list. We agree that was not appropriate. We merged all five levels into this single app, "Kanji JLPT - Flashcard N5–N1" (1.1.0), and will not resubmit N4, N3, N2 or N1.

1. WHAT THE APP DOES
Helps Vietnamese speakers learn the 1,000+ JLPT kanji (N5 to N1). Each kanji has its Sino-Vietnamese (Han-Viet) reading, a Vietnamese meaning and On/Kun readings. Learners practise with multiple-choice flashcards (kanji to meaning, kanji to reading, meaning to kanji). Progress is stored per kanji on the device: one correct answer = studied, 3 correct answers in a row = mastered. Home shows studied and mastered counts for each level, the daily streak, kanji studied today and a "needs review" list. Quizzes first revisit kanji in progress, then add new ones; at 80% mastered the app suggests the next level.

2. INTENDED USERS
Vietnamese learners of Japanese from beginner (N5) to advanced (N1): students, people going to work or study in Japan, and staff of Japanese companies. Each user picks a starting level on first launch.

3. GAP IN THE MARKET
Most kanji apps teach through English. Much Vietnamese vocabulary comes from Chinese characters, so the Han-Viet reading is often the fastest memory hook. Few apps combine Han-Viet readings, Vietnamese meanings, the full N5-N1 list and offline per-kanji progress without an account.

4. BETA TESTING
Testing so far has been limited and internal: earlier builds ran on our own devices via TestFlight and Google Play closed testing, and we are still recruiting external testers. Changes from that testing: remote kanji data corrections, a 50-question round option, one-tap review of wrong answers. The largest change came from your 4.3 review: merging the five apps and adding progress tracking.

5. STANDALONE OR SUITE
Standalone. It replaces the five per-level apps. No other app on our account offers kanji study.

6. COULD IT BE AN IN-APP PURCHASE / EXPANSION?
Yes, which is why the five levels are now merged into this app, all free.

7. CODE SHARED WITH OTHER APPS ON OUR ACCOUNT
No other published app uses this binary. Only standard frameworks are shared (Flutter, Google Mobile Ads SDK, shared_preferences, http).

8. CODE SHARED WITH THIRD-PARTY APPS
No. Written by us, not from a commercial template or app generation service.

9. CLIENT OR THIRD-PARTY CONTENT
No. Concept, branding (ManabiHub) and kanji content were prepared by us; not built for a client.

HOW TO REVIEW
- No account needed.
- First launch: welcome screen "Chào bạn!" → tap a level (e.g. N5) → Home.
- Home: tap "Học tiếp" to start a quiz; answer some right and some wrong; back on Home the progress, streak, "Hôm nay" (today) and "Cần ôn lại" (needs review) update.
- "Các cấp" (Levels) tab: progress for all 5 levels; tap one to quiz it.
- "Cài đặt" (Settings): change level, question type, questions per round; "Xóa lịch sử học" resets progress.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Works offline with bundled data; kanji lists optionally update over HTTPS from our GitHub Pages repository.
- Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), consent via Google UMP SDK with ATT.
- No login, payment, user-generated content or AI. Progress stays on the device. Same behaviour in all regions.
