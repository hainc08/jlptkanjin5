### APP PURPOSE & TARGET AUDIENCE
Kanji N4 - JLPT Flashcard is a focused educational app designed for Japanese language students preparing for the JLPT N4 examination. It provides 176 basic N4 Kanji characters through 3D interactive flip flashcards, multiple-choice quizzes, Han-Viet mnemonics, and On/Kun readings.

### ACCESS & SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
- No account registration or login is required. The app is 100% accessible upon launch.
- Typical user flow:
  1. Launch app -> Splash screen automatically transitions to the Quiz screen.
  2. Select any quiz mode: Kanji to Meaning, Kanji to Reading, or Meaning to Kanji.
  3. Answer multiple-choice questions -> card flips in 3D displaying full details.
  4. Quiz completion screen shows score summary with one-tap option to review mistakes.
  5. Settings icon allows toggling Dark Mode, Han-Viet hints, and questions per session.

### EXTERNAL SERVICES & TOOLS
- Core functionality operates 100% offline with bundled local datasets.
- Optional background update via HTTPS from our GitHub Pages content repository.
- Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for display ads (consent requested via Google UMP SDK with ATT).
- No third-party login, no payment processing, no user-generated content, no AI generation.

### REGIONAL CONSISTENCY
The app functions consistently and identically across all countries and regions worldwide.

### REGULATORY & THIRD-PARTY CONTENT
The app does not operate in a regulated industry. All Kanji data, translations, and mnemonic notes are standard educational materials compiled for JLPT study.
